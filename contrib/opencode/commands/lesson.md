Conclusion: `$ARGUMENTS`. Choose the established initiative. Capture an authored
conclusion with `kaeru_note(initiative=..., kind="outcome", body=...)`; preserve
an exact source instead with `kaeru_reference(initiative=..., name=...,
source={"text": ...})`. Relate important prior records with explicit type and
weight. Inspect committed IDs and partial outcomes before confirming capture.
